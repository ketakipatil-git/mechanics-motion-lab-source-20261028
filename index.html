THE IDEA, SIMPLY

One launch.
Two independent motions.

A projectile follows a curved path because its horizontal motion continues steadily while gravity changes its vertical motion.

NEWTON
+ GRAVITY
01

Resolve the launch speed

Split the initial velocity into horizontal and vertical components.

ux = u cos θ
uy = u sin θ
02

Horizontal motion

No horizontal acceleration in the ideal model.

x = uxt
vx = ux
03

Vertical motion

Gravity acts downward throughout the flight.

y = y₀ + uyt − ½gt²
vy = uy − gt
04

At the top

The vertical velocity is momentarily zero.

H = y₀ + uy² / 2g
